In countries where official results are published only at an electoral-district
level that does not correspond to any standard administrative unit, BLUE\_DB
codes the reporting units as electoral constituencies.
These entries are stored in \bluepath{geo/special.csv} with
\texttt{type = Electoral Constituency} and replace LAU-level rows in the
election files.

Electoral constituencies are used in 19 countries. In six, they are the
most fine-grained reporting units for which data is available:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5cm, style=sameline]
  \item[IE] 43 D\'ail constituencies and 3--4 European Parliament constituencies.
  \item[IS] 6 Icelandic constituencies (\textit{kjörd\ae mi}).
  \item[MT] 10 electoral districts for Parliamentary election and a single
  European Parliament constituency.
  \item[SI] 88 Slovenian electoral units (\textit{volilne enote}).
  \item[UK] 650 Westminster constituencies (659 in 2001, 646 in 2005) in four
            boundary sets (1997, 2005, 2010, 2024), coded by their ONS code
            (\texttt{UK\_E14000530}) or, for the British seats of the 1997
            set, by their number in the ONS boundary file
            (\texttt{UK\_P024}).  A seat's \texttt{start\_year} and
            \texttt{end\_year} are the first elections fought on its boundary
            set and on the set that replaced it; its \texttt{predecessors} are
            the seats of the previous set covering at least 5\% of its area.
  \item[UA] 225 territorial constituencies (\textit{ТВО}, \texttt{UA\_T001}
            \ldots) for 2006 and 2007, the only level at which the CVK
            published those elections.  Their outlines are rebuilt from the
            pre-2020 raions, cities and city districts they were made of (see
            the country note).  From 2012 on, Ukraine is reported by hromada.
\end{description}

In another 13 countries, electoral constituencies are used alongside standard
LAUs to report part of the results:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5cm, style=sameline]
  \item[BE] Electoral cantons (\textit{cantons \'electoraux / kieskantonen})
            are sub-provincial units used for legislative elections.
            While only older election do not report municipality-level,
            registered voters counts are only available at the cantonal level for
            all elections to date.
  \item[EE] Two special aggregate units. Additionally, 15 counties (\textit{maakond})
            are used in early elections for reporting registered voters and turnout.
  \item[EL] One special unit is used to report votes that cannot be attributed to a municipality.
  \item[HR] Three Croatian special units covering diaspora and domestic
            aggregates.
  \item[LT] One national aggregate used where a municipal breakdown is
            unavailable.
  \item[PT] Two units used for a grouping of two municipalities and votes
            not attributable to any municipality, respectively.
  \item[IT] The \textit{fuori sede} votes, cast away from home and counted
            in each regional capital (\texttt{IT\_FS01}--\texttt{IT\_FS20}),
            and the autonomous constituency abroad (\texttt{IT\_ITZZ99}).
  \item[BG] The vote abroad as a single unit (\texttt{BG\_BGZZ99}).
  \item[BA] Votes counted per electoral unit rather than per municipality
            (\texttt{BA\_BAYY11}--\texttt{BA\_BAYY15} in the Federation,
            \texttt{BA\_BAYY21}--\texttt{BA\_BAYY23} in Republika Srpska) and
            the national register total (\texttt{BA\_BAYY98}).
  \item[GE] The two districts for voters from the occupied territories:
            Liakhvi (\texttt{GE\_GEYY85}) and Upper Abkhazia
            (\texttt{GE\_GEYY86}).
  \item[RS] Polls the Serbian electoral commission ran in the municipalities
            of Kosovo (\texttt{RS\_RSKMnn}, \textit{nn} the Kosovo municipality),
            for internally displaced voters (\texttt{RS\_RSKM99},
            \texttt{RS\_RSYY98}) and in prisons (\texttt{RS\_RSYY99}).
            The last three are cast across the country and have no geometry,
            like postal votes (\texttt{resources/no\_geometry.csv}).
  \item[ME] Votes outside any municipality (\texttt{ME\_MEYY99}), cast in
            prisons (ZIKS, UIKS) and otherwise not attributed to one; no
            geometry.
  \item[FO] The national register total (\texttt{FO\_FOYY98}).
\end{description}

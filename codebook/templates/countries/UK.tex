The United Kingdom elects the House of Commons by first-past-the-post in
single-member constituencies; results are reported by Westminster
constituency.  Constituencies carry
their ONS code (\texttt{UK\_E14000530}).  British seats of the 1997 set, which
predate those codes, keep their number in the ONS boundary file with a
\texttt{P} prefix (\texttt{UK\_P024}, Bedford); England and Wales kept these
seats and numbers in 2005, Scotland's new seats take their later S14 codes.  Four boundary sets are
used: 1997 (2001 election), 2005 (Scotland redrawn to 59 seats), 2010 (2010--2019)
and 2024.

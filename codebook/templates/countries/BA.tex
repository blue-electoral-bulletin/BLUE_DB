Bosnia and Herzegovina is outside NUTS.  BLUE\_DB gives it a pseudo-NUTS
hierarchy built from its constitutional divisions:
NUTS~1 and NUTS~2 are the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina
(\texttt{BA1}/\texttt{BA11}), Republika Srpska (\texttt{BA2}/\texttt{BA21})
and Brčko District (\texttt{BA3}/\texttt{BA31}); NUTS~3 are the ten cantons of
the Federation, \texttt{BA111} (Una-Sana) to \texttt{BA119} (Sarajevo) and
\texttt{BA11A} (Canton~10), with Republika Srpska (\texttt{BA211}) and Brčko
(\texttt{BA311}) as single regions.  Municipalities are assigned to cantons
from the geoBoundaries ADM2 layer (\texttt{resources/ba\_regions.csv}).

Votes counted at the level of an electoral unit rather than a municipality
(\texttt{BA\_BAYY11}--\texttt{BA\_BAYY15} in the Federation,
\texttt{BA\_BAYY21}--\texttt{BA\_BAYY23} in Republika Srpska) are placed in
their entity and count from NUTS~1 up.

The House of Keys is elected in multi-member constituencies (one to three
seats until 2011, two in each of the twelve constituencies since 2016), and
voters can cast as many votes as there are seats in their constituency.  To
report results in a form consistent with other countries, we normalize the
number of votes cast by the number of votes allowed per voter, counting
missing votes as blank, as for Luxembourg and Liechtenstein (`fictive
voters').  The number of seats is derived from the results themselves: the
votes cast, used and unused, are an exact multiple of the valid ballots.

Constituencies are drawn as their parishes and towns.  No boundaries are
available for the wards of Douglas, so each Douglas constituency is drawn as
the whole borough and they overlap on the map.

Results are reported by raion and municipality (\textit{municipiu}), the
first-level administrative units (above the communes), with Gagauzia as one unit.  In 2019, when the
parliament was elected in single-member circumscriptions that cut across
raions, the party-list votes of each polling station are aggregated to the
raion named in its address.  No 2019 party-list results were published for
the three circumscriptions abroad; their row is the national result less the
domestic polling stations, with registered voters unknown.

Voters resident in Transnistria vote at polling stations on the right bank.
From 2019 they form a unit of their own (\textit{Unitățile
administrativ-teritoriale din stînga Nistrului}), which also takes the voters
of Bender and is drawn as Transnistria and Bender together.  In 2009 Bender was
a circumscription without votes; in 2014 neither has a row.

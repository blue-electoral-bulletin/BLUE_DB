The island of Christiansø, which is not part of any municipality, is included

\paragraph{The Faroe Islands and Greenland.}  Each elects two members of the
Folketing, and their results are part of the Danish election files, as units
of their own territory: the Faroese \textit{kommunur} (\texttt{FO\_}\ldots),
from the Faroese results service (\href{https://www.valurslit.fo}{Valurslit}),
and the Greenlandic municipalities (\texttt{GL\_}\ldots), from the results of
\href{https://qinersineq.gl}{Qinersineq}, Greenland from 2007 only.  Their
parties are the territories' own, so a Danish file carries Faroese and
Greenlandic party columns alongside the Danish ones (zero elsewhere).  The
regional views place them in their own single regions (\texttt{FO000},
\texttt{GL000}).  The Faroese 2001 polling places of villages since merged are
assigned to today's municipalities.  In the Europe-wide view the two
territories are shown with their own parliaments.

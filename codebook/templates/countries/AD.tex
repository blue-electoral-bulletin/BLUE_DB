Andorra elects half of the 28 members of the Consell General on national lists
and half on parish lists (two per parish).  Both ballots are distributed: the
national-list results as \texttt{\{year\}\_\{month\}\_national.csv} and the parish-list
results as \texttt{\{year\}\_\{month\}\_parish.csv}.  Parish lists often bear different
names from the national lists of the same party; they are separate entries in
\texttt{parties.csv}, linked through \texttt{coalition\_members} where they are
joint lists.

# Licences of the original sources

This file records, for each country, the official publisher of BLUE_DB's election results
and the terms under which those results can be reused: the publisher's open licence where
it states one, and otherwise the legal status of official election results in that
country. Sources were checked individually in October 2026, on the publishers' own pages
(legal notice, terms of use, open-data page, or the licence field of the national open-data
portal) and in the official or WIPO Lex texts of the copyright and re-use laws.

This file documents the publishers' terms and the law as read. It is not legal advice.
The attributions these terms require are collected in [`ATTRIBUTION.md`](ATTRIBUTION.md).
Boundary files are documented in [`maps/README.md`](maps/README.md) and the library README.
European Parliament results come from the same publishers as national results.

## Summary

**Open licence** (13). The publisher licenses the results under an open licence.

AT, BG, CZ, DE, DK, EE, FR, IE, IM, IS, LU, NL, UK

**Re-use permitted** (11). The publisher permits reuse, usually with the source credited,
or the country's re-use or copyright law makes the results freely re-usable.

CH, CY, ES, GI, IT, LT, MT, SE, SM, TR, UA

**Official results outside copyright** (26). The country's copyright law excludes official
documents and acts, and in most cases facts, from protection.

AD, AL, AM, BA, BE, EL, FI, FO, GE, GL, HR, HU, LI, LV, MC, MD, ME, MK, NO, PL, PT, RO, RS, SI, SK, XK

**Re-use limited to research and non-commercial use** (2). The results are under States
copyright, and the publisher permits reproduction for research and private or
non-commercial use only. Any other reuse of the Guernsey and Jersey results needs the
publisher's permission.

GG, JE

These results are therefore not part of the CC BY 4.0 Zenodo record. They are kept in the
GitHub repository (`dist/elections/GG/`, `dist/elections/JE/` and the raw files in
`output/`), each folder with a `NOTICE.md` giving the publisher's terms; the libraries
fetch them on demand, and `elections/index.csv` marks them `distribution = external`.

## By country

### Albania (AL)

- **Source:** Central Election Commission (KQZ): arkiva.kqz.gov.al (2021) and iemis.kqz.gov.al (2025)
- **Legal status of the results:** Law No. 35/2016 on copyright and related rights, Article 12(1): "Nuk janë objekt i së drejtës së autorit dhe nuk gëzojnë mbrojtje nga ky ligj:" … (b) "shpikjet, aktet ligjore, administrative dhe gjyqësore, si dhe krijime të tjera zyrtare dhe koleksionet e tyre" … (dh) "të dhënat dhe faktet e thjeshta" (https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/410069). The commission's results are official acts and data.

### Andorra (AD)

- **Source:** Govern d'Andorra results site (https://www.eleccions.ad/resultats)
- **Legal status of the results:** Llei 38/1999 sobre drets d'autor i drets veïns, Article 4(2): official legislative, administrative and legal texts are excluded from protection (https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/fr/legislation/details/6925).

### Armenia (AM)

- **Source:** Central Electoral Commission of Armenia (https://www.elections.am/)
- **Legal status of the results:** Law on Copyright and Related Rights (HO-142-N, 2006), Article 4(1) "Non-Protected Works": "official documents: legal acts …" and "daily news or information on current facts and events" (https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/15942). The commission's protocols and result decisions are official documents.

### Austria (AT)

- **Source:** Federal Ministry of the Interior result workbooks (bmi.gv.at, bundeswahlen.gv.at); the same results on data.gv.at
- **Licence:** CC BY 3.0 AT: data.gv.at dataset "Wahlergebnisse nach Gemeindekennzahl geordnet (amtliches Endergebnis)", publisher Bundesministerium für Inneres (https://www.data.gv.at/katalog/dataset/09716341-2bea-4298-9525-e936d8247d19).

### Belgium (BE)

- **Source:** Federal Public Service Interior (IBZ), https://wahlergebnisse.belgium.be
- **Legal status of the results:** Code of Economic Law, Article XI.172 § 2: "Les actes officiels de l'autorité ne donnent pas lieu au droit d'auteur." (https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/eli/loi/2013/02/28/2013A11134/justel).

### Bosnia and Herzegovina (BA)

- **Source:** Central Election Commission of BiH (https://www.izbori.ba/)
- **Legal status of the results:** Law on Copyright and Related Rights (Službeni glasnik BiH 63/10), Article 8(1)(b), "Nezaštićene tvorevine": "službeni tekstovi iz oblasti zakonodavstva, uprave i sudstva" (https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/477431); the Institute for Intellectual Property explains this as covering "zakoni, uredbe, odluke, izvještaji, zapisnici, sudske odluke i slično" (https://www.ipr.gov.ba/upload/documents/dokumenti_podstranice/brosure_prave/brosure_bs/autorsko_i_srodna_prava_bih.pdf).

### Bulgaria (BG)

- **Source:** Central Election Commission (CIK), results.cik.bg and its open-data pages
- **Licence:** CC0: the commission's datasets on data.egov.bg carry "Условия за предоставяне на информация без защитени авторски права (CC0)"; the results.cik.bg open-data pages cite Directive (EU) 2019/1024 (https://results.cik.bg/pe202604/opendata/index.html).

### Croatia (HR)

- **Source:** State Election Commission (izbori.hr)
- **Legal status of the results:** Copyright and Related Rights Act (NN 111/2021), Article 18(3): official texts "iz područja zakonodavstva, uprave i sudstva, kao što su zakoni, uredbe, odluke, izvješća, zapisnici, sudske odluke i slično … U trenutku kad … budu objavljeni radi službenog informiranja javnosti, prestaju biti zaštićeni autorskim pravom" (https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2021_10_111_1941.html).

### Cyprus (CY)

### Czech Republic (CZ)

- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0: statistical information "jsou licencovány v souladu s CC BY 4.0"; "upravené nebo odvozené údaje musí být označeny jako upravené nebo odvozené" (https://csu.gov.cz/podminky_pro_vyuzivani_a_dalsi_zverejnovani_statistickych_udaju_csu).

### Serbia (RS)

- **Source:** Republic Electoral Commission (RIK), polling-station results (https://www.rik.parlament.gov.rs/)
- **Legal status of the results:** Law on Copyright and Related Rights (Sl. glasnik RS 104/2009 and amendments), Article 6: "Ne smatraju se autorskim delom: 1) zakoni, podzakonski akti i drugi propisi; 2) službeni materijali državnih organa i organa koji obavljaju javnu funkciju; …". The Institute for Intellectual Property has held that official texts may be republished (opinion 03-47-5-877-2/19VT, 2019).

### Slovakia (SK)

- **Source:** Statistical Office of the Slovak Republic (https://volby.statistics.sk/)
- **Legal status of the results:** Copyright Act 185/2015, § 5: not subject to copyright are "informácia", "text právneho predpisu, úradné rozhodnutie" and "denná správa; dennou správou je informácia o udalosti alebo skutočnosti" (https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/22748). The Statistical Office publishes its other election datasets under CC BY 4.0.

### Slovenia (SI)

- **Source:** State Election Commission (dvk-rs.si)
- **Legal status of the results:** Copyright and Related Rights Act (ZASP), Article 9(1): "Avtorskopravno niso varovane: … 2. uradna besedila z zakonodajnega, upravnega in sodnega področja" (https://www.uradni-list.si/_pdf/2004/Ur/u2004094.pdf).
- **Licence:** The commission's EP datasets on podatki.gov.si carry CC BY 4.0, "ponovna uporaba je brezplačna".

### Spain (ES)

- **Source:** Ministry of the Interior, Infoelectoral download area
- **Re-use:** Reuse for commercial and non-commercial purposes with the source cited, under the general conditions of datos.gob.es (Ley 37/2007, Real Decreto 1495/2011) (https://www.interior.gob.es/opencms/es/aviso-legal/).

### Sweden (SE)

- **Source:** Election Authority (Valmyndigheten), "Rådata från val 2002–2022"
- **Re-use:** "All data är fri att använda, förutsatt att du anger Valmyndigheten som källa." (https://www.val.se/valresultat-och-statistik/statistik-och-data/radata-fran-val-2002-2022).

### Switzerland (CH)

- **Source:** Federal Statistical Office (BFS), Nationalratswahlen workbooks
- **Re-use:** Since 15 July 2026, federal statistical results may be used and redistributed free of charge, commercial use included, with the source credited (revised statistics fee ordinance, https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/f1zWSNpVPekdwKPMGT9dd).

### Türkiye (TR)

- **Source:** Supreme Election Council (YSK) open-data portal (https://acikveri.ysk.gov.tr/)
- **Legal status of the results:** Law No. 5846 on Intellectual and Artistic Works, Article 31: "Resmen yayımlanan veya ilan olunan kanun, Cumhurbaşkanlığı kararnamesi, yönetmelik, tebliğ, genelge ve kazai kararların çoğaltılması, yayılması, işlenmesi veya her hangi bir suretle bunlardan faydalanma serbesttir." (https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuatmetin/1.3.5846.pdf). The YSK announces the final results by decision published in the Official Gazette.

### Ukraine (UA)

- **Source:** Central Election Commission (cvk.gov.ua)
- **Re-use:** "При повному або частковому використанні інформації, розміщеної на web-сайті, посилання на джерело інформації обов'язкове." (reuse with the source cited) (https://www.cvk.gov.ua/pls/vnd2019/wp001.html).

### United Kingdom (UK)

- **Source:** House of Commons (https://electionresults.parliament.uk/)
- **Licence:** Open Parliament Licence v3.0, with the statement "Contains Parliamentary information licensed under the Open Parliament Licence v3.0".
